# MotorSkillsTest

<h2> There are 5 unique interactable items: </h2>
<ul>
  <li> <b>Lamps</b> (2), turn a single lamp on and off </li>
  <li> <b>Lightswitch</b>, turn both lights on and off at the same time </li>
  <li> <b>Carpet</b>, switches colors/patterns </li>
  <li> <b>Basketball</b>, is kicked in the direction you're facing </li>
  <li> <b>Hat</b> (3), can put on one hat at a time. Swap the hat you're wearing by clicking another hat. You can check your new look in the mirror. </li>
  
</ul>

Move around with standard WASD or arrow keys. Use mouse to look around. To interact with objects, center the object in the center of your view, and click your mouse button. Hitboxes are quite generous, but try getting closer to the object if you're unable to interact at first.
